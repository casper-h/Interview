# Interview Prep

A structured, interleaved plan for Senior and Senior Plus software engineering interviews,
covering coding, system design, low-level design, concurrency, and behavioural preparation.

## How to use this repository

Start with `schedule/README.md`. It lays out the phases of preparation and, most importantly, how
the sections below fit together week to week. The sections themselves are organized by topic, not
by time, because the schedule is what tells you how to move through them in parallel rather than
finishing one before starting the next.

Log every problem, design, and mock interview you work through in `schedule/tracker.md`. That log
is what turns this from a static reading list into an actual feedback loop.

## Sections

- [General](./general/README.md): the core interview loop, and what distinguishes senior-level
  signal from adequate signal in a coding round.
- [LeetCode](./leetcode/README.md): the coding practice checklist, organized by pattern, along
  with `leetcode/templates.md` for the small set of algorithm skeletons worth internalizing.
- [Low-Level Design](./low_level_design/README.md): object-oriented design practice, including a
  dedicated [Concurrency](./low_level_design/concurrency/README.md) section.
- [System Design](./system_design/README.md): the interview framework, the reading plan, the
  practice design list, and the white paper reading, split into a required core set and an
  optional deep dive set.
- [Behavioural](./behavioural/README.md): the STAR framework, the competency mapping, and the
  story bank to build.
- [Resume and Narrative](./resume_and_narrative/README.md): rewriting your project bullets, and
  preparing your project walkthrough, your "tell me about yourself," and your questions for the
  interviewer.
- [Mocks](./mocks/README.md): where to run mock interviews, how often, and the rubric to grade
  them against.
- [Schedule](./schedule/README.md): the phase-based plan tying all of the above together, and
  `schedule/tracker.md` for logging practice across every section.
